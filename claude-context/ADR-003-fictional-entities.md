# ADR-003 — All clubs, players, and competitions are fictional

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-08-22

## Context
Real Russian club and league names would add recognition for a small audience and would
create licensing exposure, a data-maintenance burden, and an obligation to portray real
organisations' finances and failures accurately — including the ones this game depicts
folding, missing wages, and abandoning players.

The research sources describe real people in genuinely difficult circumstances. Using
their stories as texture is legitimate; attaching their names to a fictional protagonist's
disappointments is not.

## Options
- Licensed or semi-licensed real entities.
- Real league structure, fictional clubs.
- Everything fictional, structurally faithful.

## Decision
Everything fictional. League tiers, geography, travel, and economics mirror the real
structures described in `08` §8, but no real name appears anywhere in `data/`.

## Consequences
- No licensing cost or legal exposure; no data maintenance as real leagues change.
- Authenticity must be carried by structure and detail rather than by recognition, which
  raises the bar on the writing.
- Forbids: real club names, real player names, real officials, real competition names,
  and lifting any identifiable individual's story from the sources.
