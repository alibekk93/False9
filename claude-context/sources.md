# Research sources

Reference material for the economic and social texture of Russian lower-division
football. Used for **structures**, never for names or individual stories — see
ADR-003. When an event draws on one of these, record which in its JSON `notes` field.

All sources are in Russian. Summaries below are paraphrased.

## Money and survival in the lower divisions

- Account of a *Znamya Truda* player living on his girlfriend's salary and losing savings
  in a pyramid scheme — https://www.sports.ru/football/blogs/2893563.html
- Reporting on squads playing for months without wages and what that does to a dressing
  room — https://www.sports.ru/football/blogs/589147.html
- Argument that players are never taught to handle money, and that clubs, leagues, and
  parents share the failure — https://www.sports.ru/football/blogs/2891607.html
- Background on how money entered football and what players fought for
  — https://www.sports.ru/football/blogs/2891625.html
- Profile of a player who emigrated to the Netherlands and ended up running the players'
  union — https://www.sports.ru/football/blogs/2895788.html

## Structural design note

The "illusion of choice" framing in the concept document maps to established discussion
of the technique in game studies; the DiGRA library entry cited in the original brief is
the starting point. The design's position is in `03` §7: local choices are genuinely
consequential, the global career outcome is constrained by an honest opportunity model
rather than by discarding player input.
